# STORY-009-02: SLO definition and management

**Epic:** EPIC-009 — Observability Control Service

## Description

As an SRE, I want SLOs for gateway and platform services so that reliability targets are monitored and acted upon.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SLOs can be defined and stored.
- SLO status is computable from metrics.
- Alerting thresholds can be configured.
