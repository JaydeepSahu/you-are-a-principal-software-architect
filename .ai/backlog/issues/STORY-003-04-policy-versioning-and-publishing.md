# STORY-003-04: Policy versioning and publishing

**Epic:** EPIC-003 — Policy & Governance Service

## Description

As a governance lead, I want to publish policy versions so that changes are tracked and rollback is possible.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Policies are versioned when published.
- Past versions can be reviewed.
- Published policies are immediately available for evaluation.
