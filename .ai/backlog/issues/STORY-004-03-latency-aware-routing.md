# STORY-004-03: Latency-aware routing

**Epic:** EPIC-004 — Routing & Optimization Service

## Description

As an operations engineer, I want route selection to consider latency and provider health so that user experience remains responsive.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Routing includes route health and latency metrics.
- Unhealthy or slow routes are deprioritized.
- Failover to healthy routes is supported.
