# STORY-003-05: Policy audit and explainability

**Epic:** EPIC-003 — Policy & Governance Service

## Description

As an auditor, I want policy decisions to be recorded with matched rules so that enforcement events are explainable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Decision records include matched policy rules.
- Audit events link requests to policy versions.
- Denied actions include rationale.
