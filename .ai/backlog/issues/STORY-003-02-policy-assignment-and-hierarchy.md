# STORY-003-02: Policy assignment and hierarchy

**Epic:** EPIC-003 — Policy & Governance Service

## Description

As an admin, I want to assign policy sets to tenants, projects, and applications so that governance can be applied at the proper level.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Policy assignments can target tenant, organization, or application scopes.
- Policy precedence and inheritance are documented.
- Overrides are possible with audit history.
