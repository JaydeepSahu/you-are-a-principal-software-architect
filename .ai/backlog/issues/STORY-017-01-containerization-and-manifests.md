# STORY-017-01: Containerization and manifests

**Epic:** EPIC-017 — Deployment, CI/CD & Runbooks

## Description

As a devops engineer, I want services containerized with Kubernetes manifests or Helm so that they can be deployed consistently.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Services are packaged as containers.
- Kubernetes manifests/Helm charts are available.
- Deployments can be applied to a cluster.
