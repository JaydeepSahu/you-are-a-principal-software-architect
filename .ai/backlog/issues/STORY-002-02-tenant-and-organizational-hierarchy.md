# STORY-002-02: Tenant and organizational hierarchy

**Epic:** EPIC-002 — Identity & Tenant Service

## Description

As a tenant admin, I want to define tenant hierarchies and organizational units so that governance can be scoped by business unit and project.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Tenants, organizations, and projects can be created.
- Hierarchy relationships are queryable.
- Membership and scoping rules are enforced.
