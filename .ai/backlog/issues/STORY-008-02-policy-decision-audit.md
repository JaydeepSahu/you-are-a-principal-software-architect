# STORY-008-02: Policy decision audit

**Epic:** EPIC-008 — Audit & Compliance Service

## Description

As an auditor, I want policy decisions recorded with matched rules so that governance has a complete evidence trail.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Policy decisions are recorded with rule metadata.
- Decision records are linked to request IDs.
- Denied decisions include rationale.
