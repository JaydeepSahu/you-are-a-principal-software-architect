# STORY-019-01: Budget-aware route selection

**Epic:** EPIC-019 — Cost Optimization & Budgets

## Description

As a finance stakeholder, I want routing to consider budgets so that requests can choose lower-cost models when appropriate.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Routing can prioritize budget-appropriate routes.
- Budget constraints are enforced at decision time.
- Budget-related decisions are recorded.
