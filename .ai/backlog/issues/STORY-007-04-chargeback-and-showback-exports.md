# STORY-007-04: Chargeback and showback exports

**Epic:** EPIC-007 — Metering & Cost Service

## Description

As a finance team, I want usage and cost export files so that chargeback and reporting can be integrated with billing systems.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Export files can be generated by tenant and period.
- Exports include usage, cost, and allocation details.
- Export formats are documented.
