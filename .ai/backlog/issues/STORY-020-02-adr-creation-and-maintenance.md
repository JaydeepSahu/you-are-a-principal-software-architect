# STORY-020-02: ADR creation and maintenance

**Epic:** EPIC-020 — Documentation, ADRs, and Governance

## Description

As an engineering lead, I want ADRs for major decisions so that architectural tradeoffs are recorded.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Major decisions have ADRs.
- ADRs include context, decision, and consequences.
- ADRs are indexed and discoverable.
