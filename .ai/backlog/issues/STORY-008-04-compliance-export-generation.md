# STORY-008-04: Compliance export generation

**Epic:** EPIC-008 — Audit & Compliance Service

## Description

As a compliance team, I want audit exports for regulatory review so that evidence can be delivered in the required format.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Export jobs generate CSV/JSON audit bundles.
- Exports can be scoped by tenant and time range.
- Export files are securely stored.
