# STORY-013-02: Policy and governance UI support

**Epic:** EPIC-013 — Developer Portal & Portal BFF

## Description

As a policy author, I want portal access to policy sets and assignments so that governance can be managed through the UI.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Policy sets are visible in the portal.
- Assignments can be reviewed.
- Policy publishing actions are available.
