# STORY-007-03: Budget monitoring and alerts

**Epic:** EPIC-007 — Metering & Cost Service

## Description

As a tenant owner, I want budget thresholds and alerts so that spending can be monitored and exceeded budgets can be flagged.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Budget thresholds can be configured.
- Alerts are emitted when budgets approach or exceed limits.
- Budget state is available in the portal.
